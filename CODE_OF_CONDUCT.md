# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
without modification.

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **stevenschlingman62@gmail.com**. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement

The full enforcement guidelines are documented in
[Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
